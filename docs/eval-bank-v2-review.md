# Evaluation Bank v2 — Owner Review

**For sign-off by Ambra + Coach Jon.** Draft, not yet live in production.

## Why we rebuilt it

The old evaluation was **50 identical questions**, all phrased "how much does it matter that a player…", all answered on the same 5-point importance scale. In real usage **54% of answers picked the top option** and the lowest option was **never chosen once**. Everyone rated everything a 10, so the results told us nothing about how a person actually evaluates a player.

v2 is **28 questions across five different answer formats**. Instead of asking how important each trait is (where the honest answer is always "very"), it forces tradeoffs: *pick one*, *spend a fixed budget*, *rank them*, *read the game situation*. You can no longer max everything — choosing one thing costs you another. That is what makes the scores mean something.

## The five formats

| Round | Format | Count | What it asks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Forced choice | 6 | Pick one of four traits — all equally weighted, so it is a pure tradeoff |
| 2 | Budget | 2 | Spread 100 points across five traits; you cannot fund them all |
| 3 | Scenario | 6 | A real game situation with four defensible reads; better reads score more |
| 4 | Rank | 4 | Put three or four traits in your own order of importance |
| 5 | Likert | 10 | One plain-spoken question each, agree/disagree on a concrete behaviour |

**Total: 28 items.**

## Every item


### Round 1 — Forced choice (equal-weight tradeoffs)

**1. You get to build a roster around exactly one of these. Which do you take?**  
_Format: forced_choice_

- The freak athlete who outruns and out-jumps everyone  `athleticism`
- The player who always knows where to be and reads the play early  `football_iq`
- The one with the surest hands you've ever seen  `ball_skills`
- The leader who makes the nine players around him better  `intangibles`

**2. Two players grade out dead even on tape. What breaks the tie for you?**  
_Format: forced_choice_

- The one who simply produced more — touchdowns, deflags, wins  `production`
- The one who did it against tougher opponents  `competition`
- The one who looked the same every single week  `consistency`
- The one who showed up biggest when the game was on the line  `clutch`

**3. A scout has one twenty-minute clip and no stats. What should they lock in on first?**  
_Format: forced_choice_

- Pursuit angles and how clean the flag pulls are  `defense`
- How many different jobs the player can do well  `versatility`
- How fast the player diagnoses the play after the snap  `football_iq`
- Top-end speed and how explosive the first step is  `athleticism`

**4. Your team is one piece away from a championship. What piece do you go get?**  
_Format: forced_choice_

- A closer who wants the ball in the final minute  `clutch`
- A pass-catcher who never drops the big one  `ball_skills`
- A lockdown defender who erases the other team's best player  `defense`
- A glue guy who keeps the locker room together  `intangibles`

**5. Which single trait would make you look past a thin stat line?**  
_Format: forced_choice_

- He was doing it in the toughest league around  `competition`
- He can line up and win at three different positions  `versatility`
- The physical tools are rare and can't be taught  `athleticism`
- You can set your watch by him — no bad weeks  `consistency`

**6. You're picking first in a pickup game with strangers. What do you prioritize?**  
_Format: forced_choice_

- Whoever clearly puts up points and makes plays  `production`
- Whoever catches everything thrown near them  `ball_skills`
- Whoever seems to understand the game the best  `football_iq`
- Whoever the others already look to as the leader  `intangibles`


### Round 2 — Budget (spend 100 points)

**7. Spend 100 points to build your ideal offensive weapon. Where does it go?**  
_Format: budget_

- Speed & explosiveness  `athleticism`
- Hands & ball tracking  `ball_skills`
- Reads & anticipation  `football_iq`
- Raw production  `production`
- Ability to play multiple spots  `versatility`

**8. Spend 100 points on what actually wins in the playoffs. Where does it go?**  
_Format: budget_

- Shutdown defense  `defense`
- Battle-tested against elite teams  `competition`
- Comes up big in the biggest moments  `clutch`
- Leadership & toughness  `intangibles`
- Never has an off night  `consistency`


### Round 3 — Scenario (game situations)

**9. Which decision tells you the most about your quarterback?**  
_Format: scenario_

> Situation: Down 4 with 20 seconds left, no timeouts, ball at midfield. Your quarterback has one snap and one read.

- Takes the open checkdown to stop the clock and set up one clean final shot  `football_iq · 4pt`
- Scrambles for what's there and gets out of bounds to save a second  `athleticism · 3pt`
- Calmly resets, throws it away, and trusts the next snap  `intangibles · 3pt`
- Rips it deep into double coverage for the win-or-lose shot  `clutch · 2pt`

**10. What do you most want to see from your defender here?**  
_Format: scenario_

> Situation: You're the last line of defense. A faster receiver has caught it with two steps on you and open field ahead.

- Takes a sharp pursuit angle to cut off the corner instead of chasing the hips  `defense · 4pt`
- Funnels him toward the sideline where the boundary becomes a defender  `football_iq · 3pt`
- Trusts pure speed to run him down from behind  `athleticism · 2pt`
- Gambles on an early flag pull and lives with the whiff risk  `clutch · 1pt`

**11. What's the most likely read on him?**  
_Format: scenario_

> Situation: A kid who dominated his local league all season is at his first national tournament — bright lights, better teams. Two games in, he's been quiet.

- The jump in competition exposed that his local numbers were padded  `competition · 4pt`
- It's a two-game sample; his season-long body of work is what counts  `consistency · 3pt`
- He settles in by the knockout rounds — big stages don't shrink him  `clutch · 3pt`
- He's pressing, and whether he adjusts comes down to his makeup  `intangibles · 2pt`

**12. What's the smartest way to use him?**  
_Format: scenario_

> Situation: Your best athlete is a receiver, but heading into the weekend you're suddenly thin at rusher and at safety.

- Move him wherever the game script needs — his flexibility multiplies the roster  `versatility · 4pt`
- Slide him to safety so his range erases the other team's big plays  `defense · 3pt`
- Leave him at receiver where his hands keep putting points on the board  `ball_skills · 2pt`
- Keep his role fixed so his production stays clean and easy to read  `production · 2pt`

**13. Which trait shows up on the winning play?**  
_Format: scenario_

> Situation: Jump ball in the back corner of the endzone, defender draped all over your receiver.

- High-points it and tracks the ball over his shoulder through contact  `ball_skills · 4pt`
- Out-leaps and boxes out the defender with pure size and spring  `athleticism · 3pt`
- Simply wanted it more in a one-on-one, will-imposing moment  `intangibles · 2pt`
- Secures it clean and protects it from the strip on the way down  `consistency · 2pt`

**14. Which player do you most trust to still show up?**  
_Format: scenario_

> Situation: Ugly, physical game in bad weather. Points are hard to come by and everyone's frustrated.

- The one whose tape looks the same in slop as it does in the sun  `consistency · 4pt`
- The one who out-competes tougher opponents when it gets tight  `competition · 3pt`
- The one who feeds off the chippy, big-moment energy  `clutch · 3pt`
- The one whose motor and toughness set the tone for everyone  `intangibles · 2pt`


### Round 4 — Rank (order them)

**15. Order these by what you'd scout first in a player you know nothing about.**  
_Format: rank_

- How fast he processes the game  `football_iq`
- His athletic ceiling  `athleticism`
- His hands  `ball_skills`
- What he actually produced  `production`

**16. When the competition is truly elite, rank what matters most.**  
_Format: rank_

- Proven against elite teams  `competition`
- Rises in the biggest moments  `clutch`
- Never has an off night  `consistency`
- Can lock down the other team's star  `defense`

**17. Order what makes a player worth building around for years, not weeks.**  
_Format: rank_

- Leadership and makeup  `intangibles`
- Reliability week to week  `consistency`
- Can grow into new roles  `versatility`
- Understands the game deeply  `football_iq`

**18. Two similar prospects, and you can only take one. Rank your tiebreakers.**  
_Format: rank_

- Who produced more  `production`
- Who faced tougher opponents  `competition`
- Who can do more different things  `versatility`
- Who defends better  `defense`


### Round 5 — Likert (behavioural agreement)

**19. How do you feel about a player who wins with speed but doesn't have the polish yet?**  
_Format: likert_

- The raw speed doesn't move me on its own  `athleticism · 0pt`
- I'd take him as a project and hope it clicks  `athleticism · 1pt`
- That kind of speed earns him real reps  `athleticism · 2pt`
- I'd game-plan to get the ball in his hands  `athleticism · 3pt`
- Speed like that changes how the other team defends us  `athleticism · 4pt`

**20. A player is a step slow but is always in the right place. Where does that land with you?**  
_Format: likert_

- If he can't run, the smarts won't save him  `football_iq · 0pt`
- It buys him a look, nothing more  `football_iq · 1pt`
- Being right that often keeps him on the field  `football_iq · 2pt`
- I'd trust him with the calls and the tough assignments  `football_iq · 3pt`
- That head for the game is exactly what I build around  `football_iq · 4pt`

**21. How much does a player's catch radius and hands weigh in your evaluation?**  
_Format: likert_

- I won't put him on the field for hands alone  `ball_skills · 0pt`
- Nice, but I need more before he plays  `ball_skills · 1pt`
- Reliable hands earn him a role  `ball_skills · 2pt`
- I'd design touches specifically for him  `ball_skills · 3pt`
- Hands like that win us games by themselves  `ball_skills · 4pt`

**22. A player gives you almost nothing on offense but travels with the other team's best. Your take?**  
_Format: likert_

- If he can't score, I can't use him  `defense · 0pt`
- He's a situational piece at best  `defense · 1pt`
- A stopper like that earns a steady role  `defense · 2pt`
- I'd put him on their star every single down  `defense · 3pt`
- Erasing their best player wins us the game  `defense · 4pt`

**23. The tape is only okay, but the numbers are gaudy. How much do the numbers move you?**  
_Format: likert_

- Empty stats — I trust my eyes, not the box score  `production · 0pt`
- I note them, but the tape leads  `production · 1pt`
- Production that heavy demands a real look  `production · 2pt`
- He clearly gets it done, and that counts a lot  `production · 3pt`
- At the end of the day, output is the whole point  `production · 4pt`

**24. Same great numbers — one player in a weak league, one in a loaded one. How much does that gap matter?**  
_Format: likert_

- Numbers are numbers; I don't discount them  `competition · 0pt`
- I'd shade it slightly, not much  `competition · 1pt`
- The tougher league gets the clear edge  `competition · 2pt`
- I'd move the elite-league kid well up my board  `competition · 3pt`
- Who you did it against is most of the story  `competition · 4pt`

**25. Regular season is nothing special, but he's a different player in the postseason. Your read?**  
_Format: likert_

- Small-sample noise — I go by the full body of work  `clutch · 0pt`
- A nice bonus, not a deciding factor  `clutch · 1pt`
- Showing up in big games earns real trust  `clutch · 2pt`
- I want the ball in his hands when it's tight  `clutch · 3pt`
- Give me the guy who rises when it matters most  `clutch · 4pt`

**26. A player is good — not great — at four positions instead of great at one. How do you value that?**  
_Format: likert_

- Jack of all trades, master of none — I'll pass  `versatility · 0pt`
- Useful depth, nothing I'd chase  `versatility · 1pt`
- That flexibility earns him a roster spot  `versatility · 2pt`
- I'd move him around to exploit matchups  `versatility · 3pt`
- A player who does everything is a coach's dream  `versatility · 4pt`

**27. Teammates clearly play harder when he's on the field, even on his quieter days. How much is that worth?**  
_Format: likert_

- That's not something I put on a scouting report  `intangibles · 0pt`
- A small plus if everything else checks out  `intangibles · 1pt`
- Lifting the group keeps him in my plans  `intangibles · 2pt`
- I'd hand him the huddle and the tough talks  `intangibles · 3pt`
- A player who raises everyone else is worth his weight in gold  `intangibles · 4pt`

**28. One player gives you a steady B every week; another swings between an A and a D. Who do you want?**  
_Format: likert_

- Give me the ceiling — I'll live with the bad nights  `consistency · 0pt`
- I lean boom-or-bust if the peak is high enough  `consistency · 1pt`
- Knowing what I'll get has real value  `consistency · 2pt`
- I'd build the game plan around the steady one  `consistency · 3pt`
- The guy I can count on every week wins out, every time  `consistency · 4pt`
