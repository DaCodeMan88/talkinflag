# Where the blog editor lives (and what changed today)

Hi Ambra — the blog feature was live the whole time, it was just buried. Fixed.

## How to get there

1. Go to **talkinflag.com/admin**
2. On a computer: **Blog** is in the left sidebar under **CONTENT**, and it's now
   also the 3rd tile on the Admin dashboard.
3. On your phone: tap the **☰** icon in the black "Talkin Flag Admin" bar at the
   top, then **CONTENT → Blog**. (That menu is why you couldn't find it — on a
   phone the sidebar is hidden behind that icon.)

Direct link: **talkinflag.com/admin/blog**

## What's new

- **All 29 existing posts are now editable.** Until today they were baked into
  the code, so fixing even a typo needed a developer. They're now in the
  database — click any post in the list to edit it.
- **You can delete a post.** Open it → **Delete** → then **Delete forever**.
  It asks twice on purpose, because it can't be undone.
  - Prefer **Archive** if you just want it off the site — that's reversible.
  - **Unpublish** puts a live post back to draft.
- **+ New Post** writes a new one. The slug, SEO title and meta description
  fill in automatically, and there's a live preview on the right.

## One thing worth your time

Each post shows an **SEO** score in the list. The imported ones sit at **2/6**
because they have no cover image and no "Key Takeaways". Opening a post and
adding those two things (the editor has an upload button and a "Suggest from
body" button) is the single highest-value pass you could do for search and for
AI answers.

Nothing you do in the editor needs a deploy — published changes are live within
about five minutes.
