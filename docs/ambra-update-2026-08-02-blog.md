# Writing blog posts from inside the site — a quick guide

Hi Ambra & Tika,

You can now write, edit, and publish blog posts yourselves — with images — straight from the admin area. No developer, no deploy, no waiting. A post you publish goes live on the blog immediately, and it's automatically set up to be found by Google **and** by AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI answers).

## Where to go

1. Sign in, then open **Admin → Blog** (new "Blog" link in the admin sidebar).
2. You'll see every post: the ones written in code (marked "Code post — not editable here") and any you create. Each of your posts shows a **status** (Draft / Published / Archived) and a little **SEO score** like "5/6".
3. Click **+ New Post** to start one, or click any of your posts to edit it.

## Writing a post

Fill in:

- **Title** — the headline. A clean web address (slug) is generated for you automatically.
- **Category** — pick from the list (Coaching, Profile, Women's Flag, International, etc.).
- **Excerpt** — one or two sentences that summarize the post (shows on the blog list and in Google).
- **Body** — the article itself. It uses simple formatting:
  - `**bold text**` for **bold**
  - `[words you want linked](https://the-link.com)` for a link
  - A line that is just `**A Heading**` becomes a section heading
  - Lines starting with `- ` become a bullet list
  - `![description](image-web-address)` shows an image (the **Insert image** button does this for you)
- A **live preview** on the right shows exactly how the post will look as you type.

## Adding images

- **Cover image:** use the uploader in the editor — choose a file and it uploads automatically. Always fill in the **alt text** (a short description of the image) — it helps Google and screen readers.
- **Images inside the article:** click **+ Insert image** in the Body section, pick a file, and type a short description when asked. It drops the image into your post.
- Images must be JPG, PNG, or WebP and under 5 MB.

## The "SEO & GEO" panel — how to get found

Open the **SEO & GEO** panel in the editor. Think of it as a checklist for getting found on Google (SEO) and cited by AI answer tools (GEO). It shows:

- **SEO title & description** — pre-filled for you; a counter turns green when the length is in the ideal range. Tap **Reset to auto** anytime to regenerate them from your title/excerpt.
- **Google preview** — how your post will look in Google search results.
- **Social card preview** — how it'll look when shared on Facebook/LinkedIn/X (uses your cover image).
- **Checklist** — green ✓ / red ✗ items with a tip for each (e.g. "add an internal link", "add a cover image", "add at least 3 key takeaways"). Aim to turn them all green.
- **Key Takeaways** — a few short, quotable bullet points that summarize the post. These are what AI answer engines love to quote, and Google may show them as a snippet. Click **Suggest from body** to get a starting draft, then tweak.
- **Suggested internal links** — the editor spots mentions of your other posts or of players in our database and offers a one-click **Insert** to link them. Linking posts to each other helps everything rank better.

## Publish / unpublish

- **Save** keeps your work as a draft (not public yet).
- **Publish** makes it live on the blog right away.
- **Unpublish** takes it back to a draft (removes it from the public blog).
- **Archive** retires it.

## A couple of notes

- Everything you save is only visible to admins until you hit **Publish** — drafts never show publicly.
