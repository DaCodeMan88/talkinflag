**2. You can now swap the Instagram reels yourself — no developer, no waiting**

This is the bigger change. The nine reels on the Media page used to be written into the site's code, which meant every swap had to go through me. They now live in a page you control.

Go to **talkinflag.com/admin/media** (it's also a new "Media" tile on your admin dashboard, right under Blog).

Once a month, the routine is:

1. Open Instagram → Professional dashboard → Insights → Reels → sort by Plays, last 90 days
2. If a reel there beat one on the website, copy its link
3. On the admin page: retire the weakest reel, then paste the new link in

That's it — the change shows up on the site right away. The page holds exactly nine, so adding a tenth asks you to retire one first. That's on purpose: it keeps you making the "which is weakest?" call rather than letting the grid quietly grow.

A few things built in to make it hard to get wrong:

- When you paste a link, it shows you the reel's address before you save, so you can click it and confirm it's the right one
- Retired reels are kept, not deleted — you can put any of them back
- You can type the play count next to each reel (e.g. `20400`, or `20.4K` — both work). The page then tells you how long it's been since you last checked, and nudges you after about five weeks
- If you type something it can't read as a number, it tells you instead of silently ignoring it

One honest limitation worth knowing: **the website can't tell which reels are performing best on its own.** That would need Instagram to grant the site access to your account's data, which means a formal review process with Meta that takes weeks and needs you to convert the account and file the application. Not worth it for this. Reading the numbers off your phone once a month takes about two minutes and works today.
